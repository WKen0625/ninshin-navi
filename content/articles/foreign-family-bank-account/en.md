# Opening a Japanese bank account before the birth: child allowance goes to the recipient's own account

**Open one Japanese bank account, in the name of the parent who will receive the child allowance, before the baby is born.** If you arrived in Japan recently, choose a bank that accepts you as long as your residence card has more than 3 months left. The Japanese version is at [外国籍の家族が出産前に作る銀行口座](/articles/foreign-family-bank-account).

## Why before the birth

The Children and Families Agency Q&A says the child allowance (児童手当, *jidō teate*) is paid, in principle, only into an account **in the name of the person who receives it**. It is not paid into an account in the spouse's or the child's name.

You apply for the child allowance at the ward office within 15 days of the day after the birth, and you need the account details then. Opening an account can take days or weeks (post, identity checks), so starting after the birth may be too late.

Tokyo's 018 Support (018サポート), a Tokyo payment for children up to 18, is open to foreign nationals on the same terms as Japanese nationals. If you apply with a My Number Card, the FAQ says "a parent or the child has registered a government-payments account" (公金受取口座). Once your account is open, consider registering it as that account.

## Four things that differ between banks

1. **Time left on your residence card.** Many banks do not accept applications with less than 3 months to expiry. If yours is short, renew first.
2. **Time since you arrived.** Some banks ask for 6 months in Japan. Some accept proof of employment instead, such as your employer's health insurance document or a certificate of employment.
3. **Residence cards issued on or after 14 June 2026.** The new card format cannot be used in some banks' apps yet, so you may need to apply by post or in person.
4. **Language.** Is the application in English or your language? Is support after opening in Japanese only?

## What each bank says (checked on official pages on 25 October 2026)

The order is "easiest to qualify for soon after arrival" first. It is not a recommendation ranking.

### Japan Post Bank (ゆうちょ銀行)

- Show your residence card. If it expires within 3 months of the application date, the account cannot be opened.
- The page we checked sets no minimum time in Japan.
- The app's account opening for foreign nationals is available in Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Nepali and Burmese.
- New-format residence cards are accepted in the Yucho Procedures app (ゆうちょ手続きアプリ) from 1 October 2026.
- If you cannot follow explanations in Japanese, the bank may ask you to bring someone who understands Japanese well.
- [Japan Post Bank: account opening for foreign nationals](https://www.jp-bank.japanpost.jp/kaisetu/kat_gaikokujin.html)

### Rakuten Bank (楽天銀行)

- A residence card is required. If it was issued less than 6 months ago, add a document such as your employer's health insurance qualification document.
- There is an English application page and an English phone line (weekdays 9:00–17:00). Support after applying, and the app, are in Japanese only.
- [Rakuten Bank: Account Opening (English)](https://www.rakuten-bank.co.jp/english/account/opening/)

### PayPay Bank (PayPay銀行)

- Apply on your phone by reading the IC chip of your residence card and taking a photo of your face.
- Not accepted with less than 3 months to expiry.
- If your residence card was issued on or after 14 June 2026, you send identity documents by post.
- The English page explains the steps. Enquiries are handled in Japanese.
- [PayPay Bank: Steps to Opening an Account](https://www.paypay-bank.co.jp/en/account.html)

### Seven Bank (セブン銀行)

- There is an online application only for foreign nationals. If less than 6 months have passed since you entered Japan, add a certificate of employment or an employee ID.
- ATM screens are available in 9 languages.
- Residence cards issued on or after 14 June 2026 cannot be used for the online application.
- [Seven Bank: online account opening for foreign nationals](https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/personal/account/application/online-kaisetsu-foreignnational.html)

### MUFG Bank (三菱UFJ銀行)

- Foreign nationals apply in a branch (not in the app). There is an English guide with a filled-in sample form.
- You count as a resident if you work in Japan or have lived here 6 months or more. If 4 months or less remain on your period of stay, renew first.
- Many employers use this bank for salary payments.
- [MUFG Bank: Opening an Account](https://www.bk.mufg.jp/global/productsandservices/p_banking/pb_account.html)

## Credit cards come after the account

Card payments are debited from a Japanese account in your own name, so open the account first. Card issuers screen applications, and many application screens are in Japanese only. Applying with a Japanese-speaking family member or supporter makes it easier.
